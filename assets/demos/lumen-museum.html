Room I

Water & Light

Studies in reflection and current. Each painting is built from layered gradients and vector paths — the water is a single rectangle with a memory of the sky.

Room II

Geometry & Order

Where feeling submits to the compass. Circles, bands and a rule line — the quiet discipline that every gradient needs behind it.

Room III

Night Studies

For the quiet hours. A moon that does not twinkle, a sky full of small ones, and a forest that holds its breath.

Medium

Every work in this collection is composed of HTML structure, CSS gradients, and inline SVG paths. No bitmap images, no external assets — the museum ships in a single file, the way the paintings were made.

Visitor Note

Look closely. The suns breathe, the stars blink, the fog crosses the floor of the Cedar room. The museum never closes — but you may leave whenever you like.
